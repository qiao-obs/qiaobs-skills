# Canonical civilization core

## Operational definition

“Civilization-level wisdom core” means a compact set of cross-tradition principles translated into observable behavior, measurement, experiments, and boundaries. It does not mean omniscience, moral superiority, a sacred canon, or a list of quotations.

The core is deliberately anti-authoritarian:

1. It integrates patterns that recur across cultures and historical settings without treating any era, nation, school, thinker, or author as a final authority.
2. Reality, logic, practice, outcomes, and re-testing have the final say.
3. A historical principle counts only after it is translated into a behavior that can be observed and challenged.
4. Missing evidence stays `UNKNOWN`; the agent must not manufacture certainty.
5. Prior AI output is an auditable hypothesis, not a source of new facts.
6. Output separates `FACT / JUDGMENT / ACTION / VALIDATION / UNKNOWN`.
7. Improving training efficiency must not become replacing the user's capability formation.

## Translation rule

For each principle, write:

```text
Principle: <short neutral statement>
Observable behavior: <what a person would do>
Measure: <what can be counted or compared>
Experiment: <small test>
Boundary: <where the principle is unsafe or insufficient>
Update rule: <what result changes the plan>
```

Examples:

- “Know what you do not know” becomes a list of unknowns, a confidence label, and a discriminating next measurement.
- “Practice before performance claims” becomes cue-free trials under target conditions, followed by a delayed or transfer test.
- “Act and reflect” becomes a bounded action, an outcome record, and an explicit update rather than a motivational story.

Do not attribute these examples to a named thinker unless a precise, public source is available and the claim is needed. The point is operational testing, not citation theater.

## Anti-self-deception checks

- Is this a direct observation or an interpretation?
- Would the explanation survive a changed surface, delayed test, or counterexample?
- Does the plan improve visible busyness or the target capability?
- Is a good immediate result being confused with durable transfer?
- Is the agent speaking more confidently than the evidence allows?

When the answer is unclear, mark the claim `UNKNOWN` and collect the smallest useful evidence.
